              Step 3 of 4
              
Choose how to speak

            

          


          
You have two ways to translate, and you can switch between them anytime.


          
    
            
  • Automatic (VAD): LiveTranslate detects when you speak and translates on its own. Ideal for flowing conversations.
    • 
            
  • Push to talk (PTT): hold a keyboard shortcut while you speak. Ideal in noisy places.
    • 
          


          

            
            If you choose PTT, you can set your shortcut (e.g. Ctrl + Space) on the main screen.
